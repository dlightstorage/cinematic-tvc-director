# Fictional Olive Oil Commercial

Create an initial concept and shot-list proposal only, for a fictional Egyptian
olive oil brand called Nahr. This is a test brief, not a real client campaign.

30 seconds, 16:9, digital video. Arabic Egyptian voice direction. Audience:
Egyptian home cooks who equate imported oil with quality. Proposition: care that
starts in Egyptian soil and ends at the family table. Show food being cooked and
a clearly identifiable green glass bottle. No medical claims. Warm daylight in a
practical home kitchen; avoid generic slow-motion pouring as the opening.

For this initial assignment produce one concrete concept/script and a timed shot
list. No deck, no full department package and no media generation. The director
may resolve creative choices within this scope. Ask only for truly missing facts.
